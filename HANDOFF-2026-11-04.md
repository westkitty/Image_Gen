### Images moved to the MacBook (`da1d119`)

**Why:** you said nothing should stay on the Big Mac permanently.

**How:**
1. The MacBook serves `~/images_made` read-only on port 31338, reached by the Big Mac through a reverse SSH forward.
2. The Big Mac reads the MacBook's image list every 5 seconds and streams images and thumbnails without saving them.
3. When a job needs a real file, the Big Mac fetches the image, checks its checksum, and uses it under its real name.
4. New images are generated on the Big Mac, pulled by the MacBook and verified.
5. A Big Mac copy is removed only when all of these are true:
   - the MacBook has the same checksum and size
   - no job is queued or running
   - no publication journal lists it
   - it isn't waiting to be published
   - it's at least 30 minutes old

**Install:** the permission system blocked me from installing the background service, so you ran `install-image-service-agent.sh` yourself. I didn't try to get around that block.

### Limits of the design

- **If the MacBook is off or asleep:** new images wait on the Big Mac, and images held only on the MacBook won't open until it's back.
- **Temporary Big Mac copies:** every new image or edit sits on the Big Mac until the 30-minute idle rule clears it. Any new job restarts that wait. Thumbnails stay up to 24 hours.
- **Uninstalling the image service:** images held only on the MacBook stop opening. No copies go back to the Big Mac.
